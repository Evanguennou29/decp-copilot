## Methodology

- Questions are hand-written from real markets in the currently indexed corpus (see `scripts/verify_eval_questions.py`, run against every question before it was added here): each question's `expected_uids` was checked against `data/decp.duckdb` directly, and the question text paraphrases the market's content rather than reusing its `objet` field verbatim, to avoid a circular, self-inflating evaluation.
- **structured**: montant/département/type only, no distinctive content words — measures pure filter narrowing.
- **semantic**: a content description with no montant/département/date/CPV filter that would parse — measures embedding/BM25 quality alone.
- **mixed**: both a real filter and distinctive content — the realistic case for most PME queries.
- **hybrid**: `decp.retrieval.search.search` — structured filters (on the full lot 1 corpus) + BM25 + semantic, fused by reciprocal rank.
- **semantic-only**: `decp.retrieval.search.semantic_search` — cosine similarity alone, over the same indexed corpus, no filters, no BM25 — the baseline the architecture in SPEC.md section 2 argues against.
- Reproduce with `make eval` (needs `python -m decp ingest` and `python -m decp index` to have been run first).
